Core vision & purpose

A global forum for children’s ministry leaders

Gather 2028 calls children’s ministry leaders from around the world away from the noise to be refreshed, connected, and empowered. Together, we’ll raise up the next generation of leaders so every child can know and follow Jesus for a lifetime.

Who will be there

Seasoned and emerging leaders, together

Gather brings together 200–300 children’s ministry leaders from around the world to pray together, collaborate across networks, and align our training efforts to equip leaders to disciple children.

Movement leaders

Seasoned leaders who lead and train children’s ministry movements across the globe.

Emerging innovative leaders

The next generation, bringing fresh ideas and energy as the baton is passed.

The heart of Gather

Four pieces that hold it together

Safe Haven

Deep rest for leaders serving in difficult places, with prayer spaces and an art gallery.

Next Gen

Equipping

Inspiring plenaries and high-level workshops.

Collaboration

Organic conversations between networks that spark lasting partnerships.
